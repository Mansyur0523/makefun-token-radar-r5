How a value is built impliedValue()

  1. Size curve. Least squares on log(market cap) against log(TVL) over the 92 clean comps. A dollar of TVL is worth less, per dollar, as a protocol grows — the slope is under 1.
  2. Growth tilt. One more week of the protocol’s own 7-day TVL move, held to ±25% of value. A protocol growing fast is worth more than its size alone says.
  3. Maturity tilt. 0.90 for a protocol tracked under a day, 1.10 past three years, 1.00 when DefiLlama gives no date. Older with no token is worth a little more, never a lot.
  4. Band. P10–P90 over 200 bootstrap refits of the curve, widened by the model’s own blind misses. The band is the fit’s uncertainty, not a typed-in number.

The curve, the tilts and the band are all fitted here, live, from the snapshot. Change the snapshot and every number on this page moves.

Method & backtest

Tenure: share of protocols whose token trades, by age

Age is the days since DefiLlama began tracking the protocol. Most protocols wait years, and a third of the youngest still have no token — which is why the maturity tilt is small and bounded.
